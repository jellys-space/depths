FAQ

Below, you'll find out what our modpack can offer.

Important: We recommend allocating a minimum of 6GB RAM to play on our modpack. Our Guide page explains how to do that.

What is Depths & Beyond?

Our modpack is focused on exploration and dungeoneering. This means you will strive to keep travelling and exploring new biomes and discovering new and exciting structures.

The overworld has a plentisome quantity of lush biomes; filled with unique wildlife and animals, whereas the depths of the undergrown quake with prosperity and wonder.

How do I join the SMP?

Our server is for Java Edition only, and for legitimate accounts, not cracked accounts. You will need Minecraft version 1.20.1, with Fabric modloader.

Once you have placed the required mods into your mod folder and allocated the correct amount of RAM (provided you followed the Guide page steps), you will open the game and add depths.jellys-space.vip to your server list..

Is the modpack public?

No. This modpack is not public. As this contains mods that are not published on mod sites like Modrinth or Curseforge, we ask that you keep this modpack to yourself.

You are allowed to share this website with a friend or family member should you wish to invite them to play on our SMP.

Can my potato laptop from 2001 play on this server?

Probably not, no. While expert care has been made to optimize gameplay, performance and file size; we cannot guarantee your budget or old machine can safely play on our server.

We also must note that unsupported Launchers or clients may result in crashes or instability. Such launchers include Prism Launcher and Lunar Client. We recommend Modrinth for the best support.

Join Jelly's Space Community Discord if you have issues, or suggestions. This is not an official server for the SMP, just my personal space.
